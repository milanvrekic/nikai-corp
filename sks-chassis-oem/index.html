Lightweight SKS chassis for OEM & duckbill magazines

Built around factory compatibility: it runs the original fixed magazine and duckbill detachable magazines, with no modification to your rifle.

Buy the STL on Cults3DInstant download · priced in euros

What it does

  • Works with the OEM fixed magazine and duckbill detachable magazines
  • No modification to the rifle, so it’s fully reversible
  • Includes the duckbill delete option for cleaner reloads and a modern profile
  • Designed for PA-CF / PPA-CF: stiff, light and stable in heat and cold
