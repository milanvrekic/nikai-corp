Printing

  • PA-CF or PPA-CF filament recommended; PETG, PETG-CF and nylon also work
  • Don't print in PLA, PLA+ or resin
  • Hardened steel or ruby nozzle, 0.6 mm recommended — carbon-fibre filament wears out brass
  • For PA-CF, a printer that reaches 270–290 °C nozzle and 90–110 °C bed. Settings for every material are in the README

Tools for the XCR modification

  • A mill, or a Dremel with a grinding bit
  • Eye protection
