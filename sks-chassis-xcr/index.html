SKS chassis for XCR magazines

A printable chassis that runs 10-round XCR-pattern magazines in your SKS with no separate adapter. The chassis is the adapter.

Buy the STL on Cults3D for €16.07Instant download
[PHOTO: SKS chassis for XCR magazines on a rifle]

What it does

  • Runs XCR-pattern magazines without a Hi-Cal-style adapter, with a comfortable left-hand magazine release
  • Fits Russian, Chinese (Type 56 and variants) and Yugoslavian SKS receivers, and others with the same geometry
  • Picatinny / 1913 buttstock interface, so you choose the stock
  • Bayonet compatible; under 400 g printed in PA-CF
  • Download includes all STL files and a README with print settings and assembly instructions
  • Needs the XCR rifle modification, or it drops straight in if your rifle already runs a Hi-Cal-style adapter

Printing

Best in PA-CF (270–290 °C nozzle, 90–110 °C bed, hardened 0.6 mm nozzle). PETG, PETG-CF and nylon also work. Don’t print in PLA, PLA+ or resin.
